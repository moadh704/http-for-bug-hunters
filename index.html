  1. 01Unread

    The message

    HTTP is a conversation of messages, not pages. If you can read one request and one response on the wire, every later bug class gets quieter.

  2. 02Unread

    Methods

    The verb is a promise about side effects. Broken access control often starts when the app believes the promise and an attacker does not.

  3. 03Unread

    Headers

    Headers decide who the request is for, whether it is cached, and which origin may read the response. Most HTTP bugs are header bugs.

  4. 04Unread

    Status codes

    The status line is the server's one-sentence opinion. Compare it to the body. They often disagree, and the disagreement is the bug.

  5. 05Unread

    What to notice

    You are not collecting payloads. You are collecting disagreements: two layers that do not share a definition of the request.

  6. 06Unread

    Where messages break

    Smuggling, cache poisoning, and downgrade bugs are the same story: two readers, one byte stream, two opinions.
